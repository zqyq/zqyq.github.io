About

My name is spelled like the wireless-charging standard “Qi”, but the Chinese character 琦 means a kind of fine jade — and by extension “outstanding”.

I am an {{ site.position | downcase }} at the {{ site.affiliation_full }}, where I lead the {{ site.lab }}. Before joining Shenzhen University I completed a Ph.D. and a postdoctoral year in the VISAL lab at City University of Hong Kong, supervised by {{ site.data.profile.advisor }}. My research is about making camera systems understand crowds at scale — how many people are there, where are they, where are they going, and what would they do next — and about the 3D reconstruction and scene understanding that such multi-camera systems depend on.
