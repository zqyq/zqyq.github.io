Our paper "Multi-View Optimal Transport for Multi-View Crowd Localization and Tracking" has appeared in IJCV
134(11), Article 485, online 2026-10-20. Congratulations to Kaiyi, who led this as his first author.

It is the journal extension of our ECCV 2024 work on Mahalanobis-distance multi-view optimal transport (M-MVOT)
for localization. The starting point is the same one we objected to in 2024: multi-view localization methods
supervise a Gaussian-smoothed density map, and in crowded regions that smoothing blurs the very peaks the method
is supposed to find. Optimal transport lets us supervise point-level positions on the ground plane directly, with
view-ray direction and object-to-camera distance folded into the transport cost through a Mahalanobis formulation.

What makes it a paper rather than a longer conference version is tracking. We first carry the localization
framework into tracking-by-detection (MVOT-TBD), then add a tracking-specific optimal transport loss that
supervises inter-frame association explicitly (MVOT-Track). Across the multi-view crowd datasets we tested, the
transport-based framework beats density-map supervision on both tasks.

Code is public at [github.com/zqyq/MVOT-Track](https://github.com/zqyq/MVOT-Track); the paper is at
[doi.org/10.1007/s11263-026-03093-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11263-026-03093-7).
